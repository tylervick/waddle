Two things worth knowing for whoever runs this checklist:

The issue's claim that the app configures no `AVAudioSession` checks out —
`grep -rn AVAudioSession` outside `Engine/` returns nothing anywhere in the
repo. That is not a neutral fact for a test checklist, because it decides
what the correct expected result *is*. With no session category set, iOS
defaults to one that the silent switch mutes and that does not survive an
interruption on its own. So the interruption item is written to say outright
that permanent silence after a phone call is a plausible outcome to record
rather than a surprise, and the SFX item asks for the silent switch to be
tried both ways. A checklist that just said "music should resume" would have
had the tester chasing a bug that is really an unimplemented feature.
